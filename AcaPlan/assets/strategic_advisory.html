Strategic Advisory

The Strategic Advisory function ensures that Academic Planning offers expert guidance to key governance structures at Walter Sisulu University. This function supports academic quality, institutional compliance, and strategic alignment through data-driven insights and advisory services.

A core part of this role includes providing strategic advice to the Senate Institutional Quality Assurance Committee (SIQAC) and other internal structures to ensure that academic programmes, standards, and policies reflect both national requirements and institutional priorities. Through its advisory responsibilities, Academic Planning strengthens academic excellence and enhances decision-making across the institution.

Roles and Responsibilities

  • Advising SIQAC: Provide insights and recommendations to the Senate Institutional Quality Assurance Committee regarding programme quality, academic compliance, and institutional performance.
  • PQM Oversight: Manage and maintain the Programme Qualification Mix (PQM), ensuring alignment with national frameworks and WSU's long-term academic goals.
  • Strategic Academic Alignment: Ensure that academic initiatives, programme development, and curriculum changes align with institutional strategy and national higher education priorities.
  • Academic Standards Monitoring: Support continuous improvement in programme design, accreditation, delivery, and review across faculties.
  • Decision Support: Provide data-driven insights and analysis that support high-quality academic and institutional decision-making.
  • Risk Identification: Highlight academic and compliance risks that may impact institutional quality, accreditation, or programme sustainability.

Through its Strategic Advisory role, Academic Planning ensures that WSU's academic enterprise remains future-focused, quality-driven, and aligned with the university's mission to deliver impactful and high-quality education.
