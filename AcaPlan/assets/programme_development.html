Programme Development & Accreditation

Programme Development & Accreditation is a central function within Academic Planning responsible for ensuring that all qualifications offered by Walter Sisulu University are relevant, compliant, sustainable, and aligned with institutional and national priorities. This function ensures that new and existing academic programmes meet regulatory requirements set by DHET, CHE, SAQA, and the Higher Education Qualifications Sub-Framework (HEQSF).

By working closely with faculties and regulatory bodies, the Directorate facilitates the end-to-end lifecycle of programme development — from conceptualisation and curriculum design to accreditation, registration, and periodic review. This ensures the continuous improvement and expansion of WSU's academic portfolio.

Roles and Responsibilities

  • New Programme Conceptualisation: Support faculties in designing new qualifications aligned with strategic institutional goals and labour-market demand.
  • Accreditation Processes: Facilitate the submission, monitoring, and approval of programme applications through CHE, DHET, and SAQA.
  • HEQSF Compliance: Ensure that all academic programmes adhere to the standards and requirements of the Higher Education Qualifications Sub-Framework.
  • Programme Viability Studies: Conduct academic and financial viability analyses to determine feasibility and sustainability of proposed programmes.
  • Programme Review & Re-accreditation: Lead institutional processes related to the renewal and continuous improvement of programme accreditation.
  • PQM Management: Align new programme developments with the overall Programme Qualification Mix (PQM) and institutional academic strategy.
  • Stakeholder Engagement: Coordinate between faculties, national regulatory bodies, and internal directorates to ensure smooth programme development and compliance.

Through rigorous programme development and accreditation oversight, this function ensures that WSU delivers high-quality, relevant, and future-focused academic offerings that strengthen the institution's academic reputation and strategic impact.
